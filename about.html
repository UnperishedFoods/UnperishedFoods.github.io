About

What are some likes? Dislikes?

What languages can you speak?

I speak English primarily, but I also am somewhat proficient with German and I can read a bit of Japanese, although memorizing all the kanji takes more time that I have available to me right now.

What are your PC specs?

I built my own PC with an EVGA RTX 3080 10GB LHR, Intel Core i7-12700K, 32 GB DDR4 RAM, 2 TB SSD, an 850 watt EVGA PSU, and an MSI Edge Z690-A WiFi. I have a Corsair 4000D airflow black chassis with a 280mm Corsair water cooler and 4 Corsair 120mm case fans. I have an EVGA X20 mouse and an EVGA Z15 linear switch keyboard.

Audio Recommendations?

Personally, I use the Truthear X Crinacle Zero Blue:2 IEMs coupled with the Moondrop Dawn Pro 2 DAC/AMP for really great audio at all times.
